Built with Alex Tsai and Joshua Bumatay for CitrusHack 2026.

- **Claim by claim.** Each post is split into small, checkable claims labeled fact or opinion. Posts
  full of emoji, sarcasm or all-caps are rewritten in a neutral tone first, so tone doesn't sway the
  check.
- **Every verdict has sources.** Each fact is checked by Gemini grounded in Google Search, and its
  badge links up to five sources.
- **Highlights you can trust.** Each claim is highlighted in the tweet itself, and code confirms the
  highlighted text appears word for word in the original post before showing it.
- **The overall verdict is plain code.** One false claim makes the tweet false, then misleading,
  then unverifiable; a post with only opinions is labeled opinion.
- **A public live feed.** Every check is cached in Supabase and feeds a live dashboard with D3 charts.
