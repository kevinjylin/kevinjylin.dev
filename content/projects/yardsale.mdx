Built with Rahul Palani and Joshua Bumatay at Cal Hacks 13.0, where it won a special recognition
prize in the Fetch.ai track.

Yardsale is for the student moving out right before finals who can't answer 200 DMs while selling
everything in their dorm. Take a photo of each thing and say your minimum. Claude works out what it
is, Apify pulls what it actually sold for on eBay and what it's listed for nearby, and each item you
approve gets its own Fetch.ai agent. That agent haggles with every buyer and never goes below your
minimum, or lets on what it is.

It works the other way too. Say what you want and the most you'll pay, and the deal finder searches
eBay, Craigslist, OfferUp, and Facebook Marketplace, scores each listing against what that item
really sells for, and can email the seller to negotiate for you.
