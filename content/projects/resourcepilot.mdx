Built with Kasey Doan at LA Hacks' AI Hackathon 2026, where it won the Fetch.ai Agentverse prize.

The first few weeks at a new school are a pile of small questions: where can I study right now,
what's happening this week, which dorm actually fits me? ResourcePilot answers them in one ASI:One
chat. You give it your school email once, and eight agents split up the work: one works out your
school from the email domain, others find open library study rooms, wellness events, and campus
housing, and a verifier re-checks every result live before you see it.

It never makes anything up. If a room gets taken while it's checking, the planner reroutes to
another open one. If the live data isn't there, you get your school's own booking link instead of
a guess.
