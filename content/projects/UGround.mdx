I kept finding out about concert presales the day after they ended, when the cheap tickets were
gone and resale prices had doubled. I built UGround for myself first; it turns out plenty of people
miss presales the same way.

- **Follow what you care about.** Add artists by hand or import them from Spotify, watch a single
  venue, or watch every show within a radius of a city.
- **One clean feed from messy sources.** It polls Ticketmaster, normalizes every result into one
  events table, and keeps a snapshot of each raw response so changes can be traced.
- **Alerts on real changes, not noise.** It alerts on a new show, a status change, a new ticket link,
  or an on-sale date that moves earlier. A rule's first check saves shows quietly, so following
  someone doesn't flood you.
- **Scheduling that scales with interest.** A database queue, triggered every minute, checks each
  artist or venue on its own cadence, with a daily GitHub Actions run as a fallback.
- **Alerts where you'll see them.** Discord, email or SMS through Twilio. The phone numbers and
  webhooks users save are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
