Built with Rahul Palani and Joshua Bumatay at Cal Hacks 13.0, where it won a special recognition
prize in the Fetch.ai track. It's for the student moving out right before finals who can't answer
200 DMs while selling everything in their dorm.

- **Priced from real sales.** Take a photo and say your minimum. Claude works out what the item is,
  and Apify pulls what it sold for on eBay in the last 90 days and what similar items are listed for
  nearby.
- **The model matches, code prices.** Claude only picks which past sales are the same product; plain
  code sets the asking price and the floor, and warns you if your minimum is far off.
- **An agent per item.** Every approved listing gets its own Fetch.ai agent holding your minimum. It
  haggles with every buyer, in chat or by email from a Craigslist post, and never goes below it or
  gives it away.
- **Works for buyers too.** Say what you want and your max; the deal finder searches eBay,
  Craigslist, OfferUp and Facebook Marketplace, scores each listing against real sold prices, and
  can email a seller to negotiate for you.
- **Ran end to end at the event.** Real photos priced with live Claude and Apify calls, item agents
  hosted on Agentverse, and a Stripe test payment end to end.
