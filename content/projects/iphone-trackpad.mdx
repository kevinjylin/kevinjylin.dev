- **Two native apps.** An iPhone app captures raw multitouch, and a Mac receiver moves the cursor,
  clicks and scrolls.
- **No server, no account.** The devices find and pair with each other through Multipeer
  Connectivity.
- **Two channels for two kinds of input.** Cursor motion and scrolling go over the unreliable,
  low-latency channel; clicks and pairing go over the reliable one, so a dropped packet never loses
  a click.
- **Shared logic, thin apps.** A shared Swift package holds the wire protocol and the interpreter
  that turns touches into pointer moves, tap-to-click and two-finger scrolling. The Mac smooths the
  motion before injecting it through Core Graphics.
