Figuring out what was actually happening at UCR meant checking a dozen Instagram accounts, a
few flyers, and whatever group chat happened to mention it, and I still missed half of it.
Highlander Hub pulls every campus event into one place so students can find what's going on
without the scavenger hunt.

It now follows more than 640 club Instagram accounts. Every eight hours a pipeline collects new
posts, reads every slide of each flyer with Google Vision OCR, and has Gemini decide whether a post
is really an event and when it happens before anything gets published. Duplicate posts of the same
event are merged into one listing.

Built with the builders at Highlander Builders Initiative. It started as a "why doesn't this
already exist?" question, which is usually how my projects start.
