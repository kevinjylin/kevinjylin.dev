Figuring out what was happening at UCR meant checking a dozen Instagram accounts, a few flyers and
whatever group chat mentioned it. Highlander Hub puts every campus event in one place. I'm its main
developer, building with Highlander Builders Initiative.

- **642 club accounts, refreshed every eight hours.** A Python pipeline collects new posts through
  Apify and publishes them to Supabase on a schedule.
- **Reads the flyer, not just the caption.** Every carousel slide goes through Google Vision OCR, and
  each slide's text is kept separately so a date or location can be traced to the image that
  printed it.
- **The model has to show its work.** Gemini decides whether a post is an event and when and where
  it happens, but it must cite the caption or slide that says so. Code checks those citations, and
  an answer that fails is refused instead of published.
- **One listing per event.** Reposts of the same event are merged, and free-food events are
  announced once on Discord, never twice.
