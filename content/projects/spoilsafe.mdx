Built with a team of four for CutieHack 2025, where it won Best Social Impact.

- **Receipt to pantry in one photo.** Gemini reads a grocery receipt and turns it into labeled items
  with expiry dates.
- **Reminders before food goes bad.** It tracks what's expiring and suggests meals that use those
  groceries first.
- **Built for phones.** A Next.js app installed as a PWA, with an Express backend and Supabase for
  sign-in, row-level security and receipt storage.
- **Picking the model.** We tried several before settling on one that read receipts reliably and
  cheaply enough to run on every scan.
